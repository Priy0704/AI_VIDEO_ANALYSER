Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How does VideoIntel AI eliminate hallucinations?
VideoIntel AI indexes speech transcripts (Whisper ASR), keyframe visual embeddings (Gemini Vision), and on-screen text (OCR) into pgvector temporal embeddings. Every answer generated by VideoIntel Copilot is strictly grounded against these indexed segments, returning exact start/end timestamps and evidence confidence ratings.
Can I search across multiple videos simultaneously?
Yes! With the Copilot scope toggle, you can switch from querying a single video ("This Video") to querying your entire video library ("All Videos"). The engine searches across vector embeddings for all uploaded and ingested videos in your project workspace.
What video formats and streaming links are supported?
We support local MP4, WebM, MKV, and AVI video file uploads, as well as URL ingestion for YouTube links, Zoom cloud meeting recordings, RTSP surveillance feeds, and HTTP video URLs.
How does the Human-In-The-Loop (HITL) review queue work?
If the Copilot query score drops below configured grounding thresholds, the query is automatically flagged into the HITL Review Queue. Organization admins and managers can review, edit, or approve answers before they are saved to the workspace knowledge base.
Is our enterprise video data isolated and secured?
Yes. VideoIntel AI implements strict Organization and Workspace multi-tenant database isolation. Roles govern API access and audit logs capture every user query, upload, and data modification.
